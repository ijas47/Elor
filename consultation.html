Good questions

Before you ask

Is the consultation really free?
The first session is completely free: site walk, discussion, and directional advice. Detailed lighting drawings and 3D mood visualisations are part of the paid design stage, which is adjusted against your order if you go ahead with Elor supply.
Do you work outside Kerala?
Yes. Consultations happen over video, fixtures ship anywhere in India from our own inventory, and for larger projects we coordinate with your local electrician or send our installation team, depending on scope.
Can I just buy lights without a consultation?
Absolutely. The online store and all three Celestial Lights stores welcome walk-in and direct purchases. The consultation exists for spaces where you want the whole picture designed, not just a fixture bought.
What does implementation include?
Wiring coordination, mounting, aiming and tuning of every fixture in the plan, dimmer and scene configuration where specified, and a night walkthrough with you before handover. One warranty covers product and installation.
My electrician / contractor is already on site. Do you replace them?
No. We work with them. We hand over clear drawings, supply the fixtures, and our supervisor coordinates the lighting-specific work so your existing team stays in charge of the site.
