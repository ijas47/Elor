The custom process

From measurement
to switch-on.

A custom piece is a commitment, so we make each stage visible. You approve the design before anything is fabricated, and you know the cost before we begin.

Measure

We visit the site, or work from your architect's section drawing, and record the true ceiling height, void width, viewing angles and mounting condition.

Design

You receive a scaled drawing of the piece in your space, with finish and glass samples, plus a firm quotation. We revise until it is right.

Fabricate

The piece is built to the approved drawing, then fully assembled and lit in our workshop for inspection before it is packed.

Install

Our crew handles the rigging, load fixing and wiring, assembles on site, and tunes the light at night. One warranty covers the piece and the installation.

The practical questions

Timelines, budgets
and the honest bits.

How long does a custom piece take?
Plan for four to eight weeks from approved drawing to installation, depending on scale and finish. Large cascades and imported crystal sit at the longer end. If you have a fixed handover date, tell us early and we will work backwards from it, or tell you honestly if it cannot be met.
What does customisation cost?
Resizing an existing design costs surprisingly little over the standard piece, because the tooling already exists. A genuinely new form carries a design fee that is set before work starts and adjusted against the final order. You will never receive a surprise number at the end.
Do I need to know what I want?
No. Most customers arrive with a photograph they like and a ceiling that will not take it. That is a perfectly good starting point. Bring a reference image, your ceiling height and the mood you are after, and the design work begins from there.
Can you match something I saw elsewhere?
We can design in the spirit of a reference, adapted to your dimensions and interiors, and we will tell you plainly when a reference is a protected design rather than a general style. In practice, adapting the idea to your space almost always produces a better result than copying a piece built for a different room.
Is a custom piece serviceable later?
Yes, and this matters more than people expect. Custom pieces carry the same warranty of up to 5 years as the rest of our range, and we retain your drawing and component list, so replacement crystals, drivers or lamps can be matched years later. Servicing a bespoke piece nobody documented is the reason many of them end up dark.
Do you ship custom pieces outside Kerala?
Yes. Custom pieces are crated for transit and delivered pan-India. For large installations we send our own crew; for simpler pieces we provide a rigging drawing and support your local electrician through the assembly by video.
