Why lighting goes wrong

One tube light is not
a lighting plan.

Great rooms layer three kinds of light. Miss a layer and the room feels flat — no matter how expensive the fixture. This is exactly what our consultants design for you.

Ambient light

The base layer that fills the room — ceiling lights, cove strips, diffused pendants. It decides whether a space feels welcoming or clinical the moment you walk in.

Ceiling lights · Cove LED · Diffusers

Task light

Focused light where life actually happens — the kitchen counter, the reading chair, the mirror, the desk. Bright enough to work by, placed so it never glares.

Table lamps · Under-cabinet · Mirror lights

Accent light

The drama. Wall washers on texture, spots on art, a glow behind the TV panel, bollards along the garden path. Accent light is what makes people say “wow.”

Wall washers · Spots · Facade & garden
