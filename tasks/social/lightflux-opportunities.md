## Posts for the glare lesson

Use `downlight-glare.jpg` then `downlight-quiet.jpg` as a two-slide carousel, or the first two seconds and the rest of a reel.

Caption:
If you can see the lamp from the sofa, the fitting is too shallow. Glare is a depth problem. A deep baffle hides the chip. The driver still needs a hatch. We decide both before the gypsum closes. The note is on the site: Why a downlight glares.

## Post for the facade lesson

Use `facade-graze.jpg` only as the reference frame. The caption does not say this is an Elor house.

Caption:
A facade wants two ideas. Graze close to a textured wall so the stone reads. A separate, lower light on the path, aimed down. One flood from across the yard flattens both. 3000K on laterite and timber. The coastal guide covers what the fitting is made of when the house is near the sea.
