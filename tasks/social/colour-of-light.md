# Elor social — colour of light

Source page: https://www.elorlighting.com/guides/colour-of-light
The studio behind @lightflux_lighting is www.lightflux.in. Their site teaches the plan (layers, wall wash, scenes). Those posts are in `tasks/social/lighting-plan.md`. This file is the numbers: Kelvin, CRI, beam.
Account: Instagram and Facebook for Elor Lighting (`instagram.com/elorlighting`). Kannur and Kozhikode can cross-post from Celestial Lights with the same pictures and the store name in the first line.

These posts are written from that guide. They are not captions or photographs from @lightflux_lighting. Film them in the Kannur, Kochi, or Kozhikode centre. Do not repost another brand’s reels.

Voice: one idea, plain words, a number. The last line can point at the guide or WhatsApp. Most posts should teach and stop.

Post two a week until these eight are out. Mondays: a carousel or a still. Thursdays: a reel. Stories can repeat a single number from the week’s post.

## 1. Carousel — Kelvin by room

Slide 1. Kelvin is the colour. Lumens are the brightness.
Slide 2. 2700K. Bedrooms. The warm end.
Slide 3. 3000K. Living and dining. Where most of a Kerala house should sit.
Slide 4. 4000K. The kitchen worktop and the study. Its own switch.
Slide 5. 6500K. A workshop. A security light. Leave it out of the living room.
Slide 6. Two colours in one view, and the warm one looks dirty. Pick one for the room.

Caption:
A “cool white” bulb is not brighter. It is bluer. Brightness is lumens. Colour is Kelvin. In the rooms you sit in, ask for 2700K or 3000K. Save 4000K for the place your hands work. The full table is on the site, link in bio: Colour of light.

## 2. Reel — same fitting, two colours (20–25 sec)

Film two identical pendants or downlights in the showroom, one 2700K and one 4000K, on a wood sample or a fabric.

On-screen text, in order:
0–3s. Same lamp. Different Kelvin.
3–12s. 2700K on timber. Then cut to 4000K on the same timber.
12–20s. The cooler one is for work. The warmer one is for the room.
20–25s. Elor Lighting · Kannur, Kochi, Kozhikode

Say this, or use it as the burned-in subtitle:
“People call the blue one brighter. It is only colder. Brightness is a different number. For a living room we specify three thousand Kelvin. Four thousand stays on the kitchen counter, on its own switch.”

Caption:
We keep both on in the centre so you can see the difference on your own sample. Bring the wood or the fabric.

## 3. Still — CRI

Photo: a teak sample or a silk under a showroom light, tight crop, no logo watermark from a supplier.

Caption:
CRI is whether the colour is true. Ask for 90 or above where people, food, fabric, and timber are looked at. 80 is fine in a store room. A bright lamp with a low CRI is why a door looks grey at home and right in the showroom.

## 4. Carousel — beam angle

Slide 1. The beam is where the light stops.
Slide 2. 10° to 15°. A painting, a niche, a column.
Slide 3. 24° to 36°. A dining table. A seating group.
Slide 4. 45° to 60°. An even wash down a wall.
Slide 5. A wide beam on a small painting lights the wall around it. A narrow beam as the only light in the room leaves the corners dark.

Caption:
Shape is the chandelier. Beam is the downlight and the accent. They are specified separately. If you want the piece sized to the room, the calculator is on the site.

## 5. Reel — four layers (25–30 sec)

Walk one showroom bay or one finished room. Turn layers on one at a time.

Text:
Ambient. You can cross the room.
Task. Where the hands work.
Accent. The thing you want seen.
Decorative. The piece itself.

Spoken:
“One switch for all of this gives you a bright ceiling and a flat room. Four layers, four circuits, decided before the false ceiling closes.”

Caption:
The chandelier is the fourth layer. It is not a substitute for the other three. If the ceiling is still open, this is the week to mark the points.

## 6. Still — the mix

Photo: two fittings of obviously different colour in one frame, if the centre can stage it for a minute. Otherwise a phone shot of a site where 3000K and 6500K ended up side by side. Shoot your own site. Do not pull a photo from another account.

Caption:
Two Kelvins in one glance. The warmer lamp looks dirty. The cooler lamp looks blue. Neither is faulty. They were specified apart and installed together. One colour for the room. A second colour only on a task with its own switch.

Post this from the Elor account, and send it to designers you already work with.

Caption:
For the specification: 2700K–3000K in living, dining, and bedrooms. 4000K on task only. CRI 90, R9 above 50 where material is part of the design. Narrow beam 8–15° on art and columns, 24–36° on tables, 45–60° for a wall wash. Decorative piece sized separately. Circuits and the slab anchor before the gypsum closes. ## What to measure

Saves and shares on the carousels, profile visits after the reels, and WhatsApp messages that mention Kelvin or “the guide.” Likes are the weak signal. After four posts, keep the format that got saves and drop the one that only got likes.
