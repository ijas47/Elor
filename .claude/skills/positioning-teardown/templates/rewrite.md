## What to test first

If the company has any meaningful traffic, test the hero rewrite before committing the whole page. Recommended:

- **Test 1:** Current hero vs. Option {{X}} hero. Same sub-hero, same CTA. 50/50 split. Measure: hero → CTA click-through.
- **Test 2:** Once Test 1 wins, test new CTA pattern.
- **Test 3:** Once CTA wins, test full-page rewrite.

If traffic is too low for stat-sig (under ~5K hero impressions/month), skip A/B and ship the rewrite. Iterate from sales call feedback.
