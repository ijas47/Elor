Brand guidelines · v2.0

The Elor identity:
considered light.

Elor does not sell fixtures. It plans light. The identity pairs a pearl-and-black canvas with champagne gold and caramel accents, so the warmth always reads against calm, gallery-like surfaces. Version 2.0.

01 · The mark

The Luminaire Point

On every architect's lighting plan, a luminaire is drawn as a circle with a point at its center. The O of ELOR is exactly that symbol. The wordmark quietly states the business: light, planned.

Primary wordmark · on Ink

Primary wordmark · on Bone

Standalone mark · avatars, favicons, stamps

Scales cleanly · minimum width 64px

Construction

Custom-drawn monoline capitals, not a typeface. Single stroke weight throughout (1/10 of cap height). Letterspacing is fixed at 40% of letter width. Do not retype the wordmark in a font.

Clearspace

Keep a margin equal to the height of the O on all sides. Nothing enters this zone: no text, no imagery, no edges.

Color rules

Bone letters on dark surfaces, Ink letters on light surfaces. The center point is always Brass, in both colorways. One-color versions (all Bone, all Ink, all Brass) are permitted for engraving, embossing and print.

Never

Do not add glows, gradients or shadows to the mark. Do not rotate, outline, stretch or place it over busy imagery. The product glows; the mark does not.

02 · Color

Pearl, Black,
Champagne & Caramel

Pearl is the page, Black is the feature band, Champagne is the highlight, Caramel carries prices and testimonials, Charcoal speaks. Gold is reserved for what matters: the mark's point, highlighted words, calls to action. If everything glows, nothing does.

03 · Typography

Poppins, everywhere.

One geometric sans, four weights. Light for body copy, Regular for UI, Medium for headings, Semibold for prices. Hierarchy comes from size, weight and the champagne highlight, never from a second typeface.

Display · Medium

Light is how a space learns to feel.

Body · Light

Elor designs the light for your home or business, supplies every fixture from our own range, and installs it. One team, from the first sketch to the final switch-on.

Labels · Caps

Consultation · Supply · Installation

Rules

Headings in sentence case, Medium weight, with at most one champagne-highlighted word. Buttons are pill-shaped. Eyebrow labels are letterspaced uppercase with the luminaire-point marker. Body text never drops below 14px.

04 · Motif & imagery

The point recurs.
The glow belongs to the product.

The luminaire point

The circle-and-point appears as a system-wide device: section markers, bullets, map pins, packaging stamps. Use it instead of generic icons wherever a brand touch is needed.

Imagery: always lit

Every product is shown switched on, in darkness, with its true light pool. Warm tones only (2700 to 3000K). No fixtures photographed dead on white shelves.

Surfaces & shape

Soft gallery surfaces: 16px card corners, pill buttons and pill nav, thin frame lines inside imagery, generous whitespace. Dark bands are reserved for feature moments.

Voice

  • ●Calm, precise, quietly confident. Short sentences. Concrete nouns.
  • ●Speak about light and how rooms feel, not about wattage first.
  • ●No exclamation marks, no "world-class", no em dashes, no hype.
  • ●Never promise what a photo can do better. Show the glow, then speak.
